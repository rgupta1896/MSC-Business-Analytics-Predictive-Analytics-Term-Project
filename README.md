# MSC-Business-Analytics-Term-Project-Stock-Index-Price-Prediction
Predictive Analytics - Term Project (UCL)
This repository includes the codebase and raw datasets used to develop classical and deep-learning models for the prediction of the closing price of the S&P 500 measured at t+1
The raw data includes the historical daily prices of the S&P 500 index, historical quarterly real GDP per capita, historical daily price of gold, historical daily Effective Federal Funds Rate (EFFR), historical monthly Consumer Price Index (CPI)4, historical daily price of 13-week US Treasury Bill and the monthly historical unemployment rate in the US. 
Types of models trained and tested during the study include Multiple Linear Regression (MLR), ARIMA, XGBoost and Recurrent Neural Network (RNN). Hyperparameter tuning was performed on all models, where applicable. 
The codebase is divided into 3 individual notebooks – 1) Data Collection & Pre-Processing, 2) Classical Models and 3) Deep-Learning Models
